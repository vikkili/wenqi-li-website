Mentoring学生指导

I consider teaching and mentoring the most important part of a faculty's work, and I genuinely enjoy working alongside students. If you are interested in digital cultural heritage, human–computer interaction, data behavior, or digital humanities, please write to me — and feel free to ask me for a coffee chat any time.我始终认为指导学生是教师最重要的职责，也很享受和学生共事。如果你对数字文化遗产、人机交互、数据行为、数字人文等方向感兴趣，欢迎来信与我联系，也欢迎随时约我 coffee chat。

  • What I ask for above all is a sense of responsibility — that matters more than anything else.我希望你有责任心，这是最重要的。
  • I will do everything I can to support whatever career you are aiming for, in academia or in industry. I will help you attend international conferences or find internships at technology companies.我将尽我所能帮助你参加国际学术会议、进入科技企业实习，支持你任何的职业发展目标，无论你选择学术还是业界工作。
  • Curiosity, maker spirit, and creativity are all pluses. I hope you'll bring me some inspiration too.好奇心、设计动手能力、创造力是加分项，期待你能带给我灵感！
